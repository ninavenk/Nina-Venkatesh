THE ATHLETE

Hi, I’m Nina Venkatesh, an Indian swimmer from the state of Karnataka, specialising in butterfly and competing at the national and international level.

Swimming has been the biggest part of my life from a very young age, and over the years it has taken me from local competitions to representing India on the international stage. I’ve had the opportunity to compete at major events, including the 2022 Asian Games, and race against some of the best swimmers in Asia and beyond.

My biggest achievements in the pool have come through the 50m and 100m butterfly. I broke the Indian national records in both events, with times of 27.67 in the 50m butterfly and 1:02.51 in the 100m butterfly, both set in 2026 and 2023 respectively.

I’ve also continued to perform at the highest level nationally. I won the 50m butterfly at the 2025 Senior Nationals, and in 2026 I won the same event again breaking the best indian record, swimming 27.67 in the final. I also finished third in the 100m backstroke at the 2026 Senior Nationals.

I’ve always believed that performance is built long before race day — through the early mornings, difficult sessions, small improvements and the discipline to keep showing up. Every record, medal and race has been another step in the journey.

I’m proud of what I’ve achieved so far, but I’m even more excited about what’s still ahead. The journey is still going.
