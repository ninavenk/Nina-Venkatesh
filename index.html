ABOUT

I swim
to go beyond.

I’m Nina Venkatesh, an Indian swimmer from Karnataka, specialising in butterfly and competing at the national and international level.

Swimming has been a part of my life from a very young age, and over the years it has taken me from local competitions to representing India on the international stage.

I’ve had the opportunity to compete at major events, including the 2022 Asian Games, and race against some of the best swimmers in the country and beyond.

My biggest achievements in the pool have come through the 50m and 100m butterfly.
